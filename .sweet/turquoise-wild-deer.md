Every repository now has a workspace of its own that is always there: **Home**, the first entry under the repository's name in the sidebar. It works in the repository's own checkout, on whatever branch is checked out there, with everything a workspace has (an agent, terminals, jobs, changes). It can't be archived; **Clear conversation** starts its conversation afresh. The page that listed a repository's workspaces is gone, and a repository's name in the sidebar is no longer a link.
