Agents now keep running between your messages. A message you send while an agent is working reaches it in the middle of its turn instead of stopping it; an agent can start something in the background and pick up again when it finishes; and it can ask you a question and wait for your answer, right in the conversation. Codex gets the same. Updating ends any turn that is running at that moment: send the agent a message to carry on.
