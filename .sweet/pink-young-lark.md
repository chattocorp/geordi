Your sidebar is now an outline you can write in. Add items that are not workspaces (headings, folders, todos), nest them, and edit them like in any outliner: Enter for a new item, Tab and Shift-Tab to indent, Alt with the arrows to move, Cmd-Enter to tick one off. Start a workspace on any item from its menu; it is named after it and works where the item is. A workspace under items without one still reports to the nearest workspace above it. Grouping by repository is gone; each workspace shows its repository's picture, and you can still filter by repository.
