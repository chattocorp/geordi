Your sidebar is now an outline you can write in. Add folders to group your workspaces with, nest them, and edit them like in any outliner: Enter for a new one, Tab and Shift-Tab to indent, Alt with the arrows to move. A workspace under folders still reports to the nearest workspace above it. Grouping by repository is gone; each workspace shows its repository's picture, and you can still filter by repository.
