The sidebar now shows only folders and workspaces, and a workspace’s todos are in its Plan window alone. What you write in the sidebar is a folder, to group workspaces with: it has a folder as its sign and is no longer ticked off. What you or an agent write under a workspace is a todo, as before. Workspaces of agents show right under the workspace that supervises them, and new workspaces no longer start folded. Long titles in the sidebar are cut short to one line again; in a Plan window they still wrap.
