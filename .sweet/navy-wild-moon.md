The outline works more like Workflowy. Long items wrap onto more lines instead of being cut off, and up and down go through them line by line. Rows no longer grow buttons when you point at them: the arrow that folds a row sits to the left of it, and Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux) zooms into a row, and the cursor shows it while you hold the key. Items keep their circle even when they have items under them, so you can tick them off too, and ticking one off draws a line through it. Adding under a row, removing it and zooming in are in its menu and its keys.
