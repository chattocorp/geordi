Buttons look and behave the same across Geordi. Buttons side by side, like Stop, Queue and Steer, are the same size, and those under the message box are smaller, so they no longer crowd what you write; they also show more clearly in dark mode. Icon buttons in the sidebar and in panes are evenly spaced, coloured on hover, and have a background while on, and choosing how to show changes or a file uses the same switch as Settings.
