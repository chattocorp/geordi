Sound files (WAV, MP3, Ogg, FLAC and M4A) now play in a player of Geordi's own, in the file viewer and in your agents' replies, with a waveform you can click to seek. A changed picture or sound shows its old and new versions together, the sounds drawn on one timescale, so you can compare them at a glance instead of switching between them.
