The sidebar's Today view is now about active and inactive workspaces. Right-click a workspace and choose **Mark as inactive** to put it aside for later, and **Mark as active** to bring it back; sending it a prompt makes it active again too. Your sidebar shows only active workspaces; turn off the activity button at the top of it (or press Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+A) to see your inactive ones too, quieter than the rest. The workspaces an inactive workspace's agent started are put aside with it, and the workspace you have open stays in the sidebar until you leave it. While only active workspaces show, Shift-clicking one marks it inactive. Agents that supervise your work can see which workspaces are inactive, but only you can change it. Workspaces you removed from Today are inactive.
