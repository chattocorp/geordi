Your agent's context now shows as a signal-strength icon that weakens as the context fills up, and what is left of your usage limits for the model it uses as a battery. The battery shows your five-hour limit, for both Claude and Codex, or your weekly one where there is no five-hour figure. Both turn yellow as they run low and red when nearly out; hover either for the details, with every limit listed.
