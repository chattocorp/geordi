Agents no longer make macOS ask whether Geordi may access your drives, Downloads, Documents or other private folders. The new default permission mode, **Full Permissions (Sandboxed)**, keeps agents out of those places on macOS, while letting them do everything else. The repository they work on stays reachable wherever it lives. **Full Access** is now called **Full Permissions**: choose it when an agent needs those places too.
