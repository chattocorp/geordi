Agents no longer make macOS ask whether Geordi may access your drives, Downloads, Documents or other private folders. The new default permission mode, **Full Permissions (Sandboxed)**, keeps agents out of those places on macOS, while letting them do everything else. On Linux, with bubblewrap installed, it keeps them out of your mounted drives and private folders too. The repository they work on stays reachable wherever it lives. **Full Access** is now called **Full Permissions**: choose it when an agent needs those places too.
