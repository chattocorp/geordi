Workspaces have a Run tab in the dock in place of the Run button and its menu. It lists your links and jobs, each job with a mark for how it is doing (starting, running, ended, or failed) and its port; point at a job's mark to start or stop it, and choose a job to see its output. A link turns gold once everything it needs is running. A workspace that has things to run opens with this tab instead of a terminal, and job tabs are named after their labels. The `default` setting in `.geordi.yml` is no longer used; files that have it still work.
