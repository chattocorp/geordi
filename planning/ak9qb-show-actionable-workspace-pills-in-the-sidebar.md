title: Show actionable workspace pills in the sidebar
---
The sidebar shows the primary merge, repair, review, or archive action on hover and keyboard focus. A browser story verifies that the pills appear and receive focus. The whole pill accepts clicks and briefly shows a checkmark in the action icon’s place. The story checks hit targets at both ends and in the center. Rows without an action pill keep their status on hover. The separate archive icon was removed.

Workspace rows without a status icon keep the action area empty on hover and keyboard focus.
