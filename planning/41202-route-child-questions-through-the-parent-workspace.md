A child can ask its parent for a decision during a delegated turn. The parent can answer directly or forward the form to the person. A browser check verified two outstanding requests across a forge restart and an answer saved against the correct child. Tests cover direct-parent permissions, concurrent requests, cancellation, and answer retries.
